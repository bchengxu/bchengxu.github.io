## Overview

CoFEH interleaves LLM-driven feature engineering and Bayesian hyperparameter optimization. A tree-of-thought feature engineering optimizer, a Bayesian optimization module, and a dynamic optimizer selector cooperate through mutual conditioning to improve end-to-end AutoML.
