## Overview

LB-MCTS combines large language models and Bayesian optimization within a shared Monte Carlo tree search framework for combined algorithm selection and hyperparameter optimization (CASH). A reliability-aware policy balances semantic proposals from an LLM with quantitative proposals from Bayesian optimization as the search progresses.
